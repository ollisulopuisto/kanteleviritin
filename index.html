Tuning Guide / Viritysohje

1. Base tuning at ♮ (Perusviritys C-duurissa): Always set all 7 concert kantele levers (koneisto) to neutral (♮) before turning tuning pins. Click Neutral / C-duuri (Kaikki ♮) to match the tuner to neutral.

2. New wound bass strings (H₁–H): Freshly replaced bass strings stretch noticeably at first. Re-check the lowest octave (H₁ through H) frequently during the first few weeks and make a second pass from bass to treble after the soundboard settles.

3. Soft-strung vintage kanteles (Pehmeäkielinen soitin): Instruments such as 1960s Oiva Heikkilä concert kanteles have gentler string tension. Turn the tuning key in small 5°–10° increments, and always approach the target pitch from slightly flat (loosen slightly if sharp, then tighten clockwise / kiristä).

4. Checking concert levers (Koneiston tarkistus): Flip levers smoothly and avoid unnecessary extreme positions (such as H→His) when not required by the piece.
